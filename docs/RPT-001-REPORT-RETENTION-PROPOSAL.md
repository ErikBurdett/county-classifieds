# Listing Report Retention Proposal

**Status: proposal — pending legal and product approval**  
**Not an accepted retention policy. Do not implement deletion from this document.**

## Proposed direction

Retain listing reports and immutable triage actions only as long as needed for
marketplace safety operations, repeat-abuse investigation, legal obligations,
and approved deletion requirements. Review whether report descriptions, private
contact email, HMACed source identifiers, and staff notes need different
retention periods and access controls.

## Decisions still required

- Legal retention period and deletion/erasure exceptions by data element.
- Whether HMAC key rotation must preserve historical rate-limit linkage.
- Reporter access, correction, or deletion request handling.
- Escalation evidence, law-enforcement request, and litigation-hold process.
- Staff access reviews and export/audit requirements.
- Whether resolved and dismissed reports have different retention periods.

Until approval, the implementation preserves records and exposes them only to
authorized staff. This document intentionally sets no retention duration.
