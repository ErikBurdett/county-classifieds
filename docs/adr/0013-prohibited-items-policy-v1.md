# ADR-0013: Prohibited-items policy version 1

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-07-23  
**Owner:** Project owner

## Decision
TheCountyPost Market prohibits weapons/firearms, controlled substances, adult services, financial/crypto offers, scams, stolen/counterfeit goods, and trafficking/exploitation. Suspected illegal activity must be escalated to staff.

## Consequences
Moderation uses stable version-1 reason codes and preserves their historical references. A deterministic keyword scanner may flag title/description for review but never automatically rejects or asserts that content is illegal. Staff decide outcomes and retain internal notes separately from seller-facing explanations. Future policy changes require a new versioned record and, when behavior materially changes, a superseding ADR.
